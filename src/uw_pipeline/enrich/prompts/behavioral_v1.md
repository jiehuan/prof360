Task: behavioral insights from the earnings call transcripts (T items, oldest first).

Compare management's language across the calls and report what changed and why an underwriter
should care:
- Tone Shift: confidence vs caution, defensiveness in Q&A, evasive answers.
- Strategic Focus Shift: priorities newly emphasised or dropped.
- Emerging Risk Language: risks, terms or issues that appear for the first time or grow
  (e.g. "restructuring", "challenging macro", litigation, regulatory scrutiny).
- Wording Change: guidance or key phrases that softened or hardened.

Each insight cites quotes from at least two different calls when it is about a change (one
quote may be from the call where something first appears). Report 2 to 6 insights; report none
if there is only one call or nothing meaningful changed.
