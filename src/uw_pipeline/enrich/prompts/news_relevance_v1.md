You screen a news feed for credit and D&O underwriters reviewing one company. For each article
(headline and summary) decide whether it is news ABOUT that company.

Verdicts:
- SUBJECT: the company is a main subject: its results, guidance, deals, financing, products,
  operations, management or board, litigation, regulation, labor, incidents, strategy, or a
  substantive analysis of the company itself.
- PASSING: the company is only mentioned in passing (a list of peers, a market wrap, another
  company's story, an event it merely sponsors or hosts).
- ANALYST: the company appears only as the analyst or broker giving a rating, target or view on
  another company.
- HOLDING: a fund or investor bought or sold the company's shares, or the company bought or sold
  another company's shares (13F-style holding reports).
- MARKET: only the company's share price moved, with no company news behind it ("stock rises
  0.9%", "what to watch before earnings").

Judge from the text given; when unsure between SUBJECT and another verdict, choose SUBJECT.
Return a verdict for every id, using the ids exactly as given.
