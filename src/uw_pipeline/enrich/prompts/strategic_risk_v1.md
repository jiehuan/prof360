Task: strategic and risk insights for three categories.
- Governance & Leadership: executive and board changes, auditor, capital allocation choices
  (e.g. buybacks with negative equity), compensation or control issues.
- Risk & Legal: litigation and regulatory matters, internal controls, going concern, balance
  sheet or liquidity stress, risk factors that are new or specific to this company.
- Event Signals: material events (M&A, impairments, restatements, listing changes, debt events).

Use the rule-based signals (S items) as the starting point and the filing excerpts (E items) and
transcripts for context. Signals marked [OVERRULED] or [DISPUTED] are uncertain: mention one only
if other evidence supports it, and say it is unconfirmed. Prefer company-specific risk factors
over boilerplate that applies to every company.

Report up to 12 insights. For a category with nothing material, add it to `no_signal` with a
one-sentence rationale instead of inventing an insight.
