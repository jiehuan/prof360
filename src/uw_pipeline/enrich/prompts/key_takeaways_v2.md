Task: the Key Takeaways, 2 or 3 bullet points that a professional liability underwriter should
read first about this company. They sit above the Key Insights, are read in seconds and are
shown in a narrow box: every bullet must fit in 100 characters.

Draw on everything below: the key insights (K items), the strategic/risk and behavioral
insights (R and B items), the signals (S items) and the metrics (M items). Choose what matters
most for professional lines, in this order:
1. Directors and officers (D&O): securities class action and derivative exposure (share-price
   drops, earnings misses, guidance cuts, disclosure or accounting problems, restatements,
   auditor changes), regulatory investigations and enforcement, litigation, governance and
   leadership changes, insider selling, financial distress (leverage, liquidity, refinancing),
   M&A.
2. Errors and omissions, cyber incidents, and employment practices, where the input shows them.

Each takeaway:
- `topic`: at most 5 words naming the exposure, e.g. "Securities litigation exposure".
- `text`: one short sentence of at most 100 characters, counting spaces and punctuation
  (about 15 words): the specific fact, with its figure or event, and the exposure it creates.
  Plain language; standard abbreviations (D&O, SEC, CFO, $1.2B, Q2 2026) save room. A bullet
  over 100 characters is not shown: if a point does not fit, state it more narrowly or choose
  another point.
- `evidence`: quotes from the items it rests on (usually K, R or S items; metric rows can be
  quoted as written).

Order from most to least important. Each takeaway makes a different point and does not repeat a
key insight word for word. Name a mitigating strength only when it materially changes the
picture. No links or source names in the text.
