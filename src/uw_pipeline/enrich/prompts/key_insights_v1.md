Task: the Key Insights summary, 6 to 10 insights that an underwriter should read first.

Draw on the metrics (trends across the listed periods), the strategic/risk and behavioral
insights from earlier steps (R and B items), the signals and the news. Cover the company's
financial condition (growth, profitability, leverage, liquidity, or for financial institutions
capital, liquidity and credit quality) as well as governance, legal and event risks.

Each insight:
- `type`: Warning (a risk to watch), Negative (an adverse fact), Positive (a mitigating
  strength), or Info (relevant context).
- `title`: at most 8 words.
- `summary`: two or three sentences.
- `uw_relevance`: one or two sentences on the D&O angle.
- `supporting_evidence`: the key facts, briefly.
- `related_metric_codes`: codes of metrics it relies on (e.g. debt_to_equity), may be empty.
- `evidence`: quotes from the cited items (metric rows can be quoted as written).
Order from most to least important. Do not repeat the same point in two insights.
